Our story: from a small passion in Bhaktapur to Nepal's leading garden studio

Sewa Subidha Gardening started more than a decade ago with a simple conviction — every outdoor space in Nepal holds untapped beauty. What began as a small landscaping operation in Bhaktapur has grown into the Kathmandu Valley's most comprehensive garden design and maintenance studio, serving homeowners in Kathmandu, Lalitpur, Bhaktapur, Madhyapur Thimi, and select projects in Pokhara and Chitwan.

Our team of certified horticulturists, landscape architects, and skilled craftspeople has built hundreds of gardens — from intimate courtyard sanctuaries in Patan to sprawling resort grounds in Nagarkot. Each project has sharpened our understanding of Nepal's unique climate, soil conditions, and cultural aesthetic.

Our full range of services

Unlike smaller operators who focus on a single service, Sewa Subidha Gardening offers end-to-end garden solutions under one roof:

  • Garden Design & Landscaping: Full concept-to-completion landscape architecture — design, planning, and building from scratch.
  • Lawn Installation: Sodding, seeding, and complete lawn preparation for thick, lush lawns suited to Nepal's climate.
  • Plant Selection & Planting: Expert curation of plants matched to your microclimate, soil, and aesthetic.
  • Routine Maintenance: Weekly, fortnightly, and monthly plans including mowing, pruning, fertilising, and seasonal care.
  • Smart Irrigation Setup: Efficient drip irrigation and sprinkler systems calibrated for Nepal's water supply realities.
  • Event & Wedding Décor: Breathtaking floral garden arrangements for weddings, receptions, and corporate events.
