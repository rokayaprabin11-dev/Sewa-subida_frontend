Our Story

Rooted in Passion,
Grown Through Purpose

Sewa Subidha was born from a simple belief: that every person deserves a beautiful, living outdoor space. In 2014, founder Ramesh Shrestha planted the seed of that vision with little more than a pair of pruning shears and an unwavering love for horticulture in the heart of Godawari -7, Lalitpur.

What started as a small community garden project quickly blossomed. Word spread about our meticulous craftsmanship, our respect for each client's unique vision, and our deep commitment to sustainable practices. Within three years, we had outgrown our humble beginnings and built a full team of dedicated landscape designers and master gardeners.

A garden is not built in a day — it is a conversation between the soil, the seasons, and the souls who tend it.

— Ramesh Shrestha, Founder & Lead Designer

Today, Sewa Subidha is Nepal's most trusted landscaping studio — with hundreds of residential gardens, corporate campuses, and community green spaces to our name. But our roots remain the same: we show up, we listen, and we grow something extraordinary together.

Our Values

The Principles That
Guide Every Garden

Everything we do is guided by a deep respect for nature, our clients, and the communities we work in.

01

Sustainability First

We design with the ecosystem in mind — selecting drought-tolerant natives, building living soils, and minimising chemical inputs so your garden thrives for generations, not just seasons.

02

Artistry in Every Detail

Landscaping is art. We obsess over plant textures, seasonal colour palettes, negative space, and the way light moves through a garden at golden hour. Beauty is never an afterthought.

03

Partnerships, Not Projects

We don't just complete jobs — we build long-term relationships. Our clients become partners, and their gardens become portfolios we are proud to tend, season after season.

04

Science Meets Soul

Every planting plan is backed by soil analysis, microclimate studies, and horticultural science. Yet we never lose the intuitive, human touch that transforms a yard into a sanctuary.

05

Honest Communication

No hidden costs. No vague timelines. We keep you informed every step of the way — from the first site visit to the final planting, you always know exactly where your project stands.

06

Community Roots

We invest in our community through school garden programs, public greening initiatives, and apprenticeships for young horticulturists — because a greener neighbourhood benefits everyone.
