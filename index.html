FAQ

Got Questions?

How long does a garden project take?
+

Most residential garden projects take between 3 to 14 days depending on scope. We will give you a precise, written timeline during your free consultation before any work begins. Larger commercial projects are scoped individually.

Do you provide weekly maintenance plans?
+

Yes! We offer flexible weekly, fortnightly, and monthly maintenance plans. All plans include mowing, edging, pruning, and seasonal fertilisation. Plans can be customised and paused anytime.

Are your quotes really free?
+

Absolutely. All initial consultations and site visits are completely free with no obligation. We visit your space and provide a fully itemised quote within 48 hours of the visit.

Do you work on commercial properties?
+

Yes — we handle both residential and commercial projects including office parks, hotels, wedding venues, restaurants, and corporate campuses. Our commercial team operates 6 days a week.

What areas of Nepal do you serve?
+

We primarily serve the Kathmandu Valley — Kathmandu, Lalitpur, Bhaktapur, and Madhyapur Thimi. We also take select projects in Pokhara and Chitwan. Contact us for availability outside these areas.

Still have questions?

Our team is ready to answer anything — from plant selection advice to full project quotes. Reach out today and we will respond within 2 hours.
